Welcome to NEXUS Mining Protocol. To start earning rewards, connect your wallet, choose a mining package tier from the Packages menu, and deploy nodes securely.

1. How does a blockchain fund equity pool generate revenue?

Blockchain staking pool systems are automated software programs that manage and execute contracts according to predefined rules. Users pay a licensing fee to access the system, granting access to blockchain functionality and earning daily returns through Liquidity Pool returns and staking rewards.

2. What are non-pledged and pledged rights?

Non-staking DeFi equity funds simply require you to deposit cryptocurrency into your wallet. Staking DeFi equity funds require you to stake cryptocurrency into an equity pool and earn higher returns through smart contracts.

3. How to calculate daily earnings?

After successful joining, the system calculates the amount of USDT you hold via smart contract. Daily percentage rates depend on the chosen mining tier package.

4. How do I withdraw my money?

You need to convert your mined ETH into USDT and then start withdrawing through the Account page. Ensure you have some ETH for gas fees on the Sepolia network.
